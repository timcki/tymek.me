After years of using various blogging platforms and content management systems, I've finally settled on static site generators for my personal site. Here's why.

## The Benefits

### Speed

Static sites are incredibly fast. There's no database to query, no server-side processing - just plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript served directly to visitors.

### Security

With no database or server-side code execution, the attack surface is minimal. No WordPress vulnerabilities to worry about!

### Version Control

Since everything is just files, I can use Git to track changes, roll back mistakes, and collaborate with others.

### Cost

Hosting static sites is cheap or even free. You can use services like:

- GitHub Pages
- Netlify
- Vercel
- Cloudflare Pages

## The Trade-offs

Of course, static sites aren't perfect for every use case:

- No built-in comments (but you can use third-party services)
- Content updates require rebuilding
- Not ideal for frequently changing content

But for a personal blog? Static sites are perfect.

## Conclusion

For my needs - a simple blog to share ideas and projects - static site generators offer the perfect balance of simplicity, performance, and control.
