I recently discovered Zola, a static site generator written in Rust, and I'm impressed by its simplicity and speed.

## Why Zola?

Zola is a fast, simple static site generator that's perfect for personal blogs and documentation sites. Here are some reasons I chose it:

- **Fast**: Written in Rust, it builds sites incredibly quickly
- **Simple**: Easy to set up and configure
- **Flexible**: Uses Tera templating which is powerful yet approachable
- **Actively maintained**: Regular updates and improvements

## Getting Started

Setting up a new Zola site is straightforward:

```bash
zola init my-site
cd my-site
zola serve
```

And that's it! You now have a working site running locally.
